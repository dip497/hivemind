## Before you start

- On the same network, you need nothing else.
- From another network, you need your own [network server](/self-hosting).
- To let a person into a workspace, open that workspace's window first.

## Pair a device or let someone in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the invite">
    Open **Settings → Connect** and press **Connect**.

    Check: you see a QR code, six words and **Copy the link**. The invite lasts a day, or until one device uses it, or until you make another.

    The six words work on the same network only. From elsewhere, send the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it on the other device">
    | Device | Do |
    |---|---|
    | Another computer | Press **Have a code? Join**, on Home or in **Settings → Connect**. Paste the link or type the words, and press **Connect**. |
    | A phone | Pick the **Phone browser** tab first, then scan the code. See [Answer agents from your phone](/phone). |

    Check: both screens show the same three words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer on your computer">
    Answer only if the three words match.

    | Answer | Result |
    |---|---|
    | **Deny** | Nothing is kept. Closing the notice, or waiting about three minutes, is the same. |
    | **Someone else**, with a role | That person joins this workspace at **Can view**, **Can edit board** or **Can use terminals**. |
    | **It's me — the words match** | After **Yes** on the other device, it is paired as you. Your workspaces are its workspaces. |

    **Someone else** is not offered for a phone, or for an invite made from Home. **It's me** is not offered for a computer that already shares a workspace with someone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it worked

**Settings → Connect → Connected → Your devices** lists each paired device as **Always on** (a host), **A computer**, or **Phone or browser**. A person you let in shows under **People in this workspace**.

## If it didn't

| You see | Do |
|---|---|
| `This invite has expired or was used. Ask for a new one.` | Press **Connect** again for a new invite. |
| `This invite was not made for this device.` | Make a new invite and use it on this device. |
| The six words are refused from another network | Send the link instead. It needs your own [network server](/self-hosting). |

More in [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
