title: "Connect devices"
description: "Pair your phone or another computer, or let someone in, with one offer from Settings → Connect."
---

One offer from **Settings → Connect** serves your phone, another computer of yours, or another
person. You decide which when the other device uses it.

<Prompt description="Paste this into your coding agent to check that two of your computers can connect, then pair them." actions={["copy","cursor"]}>
Help me pair another computer of mine with this one in Hivemind, so its workspaces are mine on both.

1. Check Hivemind is installed here: `hive --version`. Ask me whether the other computer has it too; if not, tell me the install command (`bash <(curl -fsSL https://hivemind.griiken.com/install.sh)` on Linux and macOS, `irm https://hivemind.griiken.com/install.ps1 | iex` in PowerShell on Windows).
2. Run `hive network show` here. If it says Local network, the six words work only when both computers are on the same network; ask me whether they are. If they are not, tell me a network server is needed first (see the "Your own network server" page) and stop.
3. If this computer is on a network server, run `hive network doctor` and check every relay answers; ask me to run `hive network show` on the other computer and confirm it names the same network.
4. Then walk me through the pairing, which only I can do in the app: on this computer, Settings → Connect → Connect; on the other one, Have a code? Join, then paste the link or type the six words and press Connect; both screens show three words; if they match, I press "It's me — the words match" here and Yes on the other computer.

At the end, ask me to confirm the other computer is listed under Connected → Your devices on Settings → Connect, and tell me Unpair there cuts it off.
</Prompt>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make an offer">
    Open **Settings → Connect** and press **Connect**. To let a person into a workspace, do it in
    that workspace's window. The offer shows a QR code, six words and **Copy the link**. It lasts a
    day, until one device uses it, or until you make another.

    The six words work on the same network only. From elsewhere, send the link; that needs a
    [network server](/self-hosting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it on the other device">
    | Device | Do |
    |---|---|
    | Another computer | **Have a code? Join** (Home or Settings → Connect), paste the link or type the words, **Connect**. |
    | A phone | Pick the **Phone browser** tab first, then scan the code: [Phone and browser](/phone#pair). |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer on your computer">
    Both screens show three words. Answer only if they match.

    | Answer | Result |
    |---|---|
    | **Deny** | Nothing is kept. Closing the notice, or waiting about three minutes, is the same. |
    | **Someone else** + a role | That person joins this workspace at **Can view**, **Can edit board** or **Can use terminals**. |
    | **It's me — the words match** | After **Yes** on the other device, it is paired as you: your workspaces are its workspaces. |

    **Someone else** is not offered for a phone or for an offer made from Home. **It's me** is not
    offered for a computer that already shares a workspace with someone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your devices

**Connected → Your devices** lists each paired device: *Always on* (a host), *A computer*, or
*Phone or browser*. **Unpair** cuts it off from this computer. A host pairs from its own terminal:
`hive host pair` ([Always-on host](/host)).
