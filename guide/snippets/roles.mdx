Each role can do everything the roles above it can. People and programs get the same four roles.

| Role | For a person, the app says | For a token, **API access** says | Can |
|---|---|---|---|
| `view` | **Can view** | **View** | see the canvas, terminals, agents and their replies, files, diffs, issues and review comments |
| `edit` | **Can edit board** | **Edit** | also move, add and close tiles, press widgets, open a tool's tile |
| `terminals` | **Can use terminals** | **Use terminals** | also type into terminals (a person once given the keyboard) |
| `agents` | **Can drive agents** | **Drive agents** | also start, answer, message, put to sleep and close agents, which means running commands on your computer. A person gets it only while they are here, after a warning. |
