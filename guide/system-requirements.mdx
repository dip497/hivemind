The systems each release is built for, and what you get on each.

| System | You get | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 | the app, `hive` and `hive-net` | The installer unpacks the app, so it needs no `libfuse2`. |
| macOS on Apple Silicon | the app, `hive` and `hive-net` | macOS 13 or later. The app is not signed by Apple; the installer clears the quarantine mark. |
| Windows x64 | the app, `hive` and `hive-net` | Built and checked automatically for each release, but not yet tested on real hardware. [Report what breaks](https://github.com/dip497/hivemind/issues). |
| Linux arm64 | `hive` and `hive-net` only | No app. Use it as a [machine](/machines) that another computer drives. |
| macOS on Intel | nothing prebuilt | Build from source with `bash install.sh --dev`. |

## What else you need

- **To install:** `curl` and `bash` on Linux and macOS, or PowerShell 5.1 or newer on Windows.
- **To run agents:** each coding agent's own program, such as `claude` or `codex`, on your `PATH`. No agent ships in the app.
- **To build from source:** git, node 22 or newer, pnpm 12 or newer, and bun.
- **To reach other machines:** `ssh`, with access to each machine.
- **For your phone, or devices on other networks:** your own [network server](/self-hosting).
