Hivemind runs on your own computers. This page says what can reach what, and what leaves your
machine.

<Diagram name="trust-boundary" caption="What can reach what" />

- **Runs as you**: **agents**, in your terminals, and **tools**, inside the app. They reach your files, programs, logins and network.
- **Sandbox**: **views**, in a frame with no Node, and **widgets**, in a worker. No files, no network. They reach **the workspace** only through the permissions they asked for.
- **Guests** come in with a [role](/multiplayer#roles), **your programs** with an [API token](/api-tokens). A call the role does not allow is refused before it runs.
- **Your server** sees only encrypted traffic: see [Encrypted end to end](/how-devices-connect#encrypted-end-to-end).
- **HiveHub** files are checked before they install: see below.

## Permissions

- A view or widget lists its permissions in its file. You see them in words, under **What it can do**, before you install it. An unknown one is refused.
- Each message it sends is checked against them. With `workspace:prompt`, you also confirm each prompt it writes.
- What each permission allows: [Permissions](/extension-authoring#permissions). What else the sandbox stops: [The sandbox](/extension-authoring#the-sandbox).

## Sites in a Browser tile

A site in a Browser tile asks before it uses your camera, microphone or location, shows
notifications, or opens a link in another program (`mailto:`, `zoommtg:`, `slack:`). The question
shows over the top of the page and names the site and what it wants, with **Block** and **Allow**.
They take a click once the question has been on screen for half a second, so a click meant for the
page cannot answer it. Your answer holds for that site until you quit the app. A question you leave
for a minute, or whose tab you close, is a no.

The **Site permissions** button by the address lists what the site you are on may use: notifications, the camera,
the microphone, the location, and anything else you answered. Choose **Allow**, **Block** or
**Ask** for each. Allow a site that only checks whether it may and never asks, or take back a
**Block**.

As in Chrome, a site does not ask to go full screen, copy, hold the pointer or the keys (holding
**Esc** gives them back), keep the screen on, or keep its data when the disk runs short. A site never
opens a link that runs code or opens files here (`file:`, `javascript:`, `search-ms:`), and never
gets your USB, serial or input devices, a speaker or your screen: the app has no chooser for them.
A view, or a page a tool shows in its tile, never gets any of these. A tool's own code runs in the
app's page and gets what the app uses: notifications and copying.

<Warning>
With **Agent browser control** on, an agent drives Browser tiles over the Chrome DevTools Protocol,
and through it can give a site any of these without asking you. Switch it on only for agents you
trust with your browser.
</Warning>

## Tools start switched off

A tool you install starts switched off, and you turn it on yourself. The tools that ship with
the app start on. If a tool breaks the app, start it with `HIVEMIND_SAFE_MODE=1 hivemind`: only
the tools that ship with the app load.

## What you install from HiveHub

<Tooltip tip="The catalogue Hivemind installs agents and plugins from." cta="Glossary" href="/glossary#hivehub">HiveHub</Tooltip> lists every file of a plugin with its hash, and Hivemind checks each file before it installs: see [Files stay in the author's repository](/hivehub#files-stay-in-the-authors-repository).

- `hive agents install`, from HiveHub or from a repository, prints what the agent runs first. It installs only when you run it again with `--yes`.
- When you install from HiveHub, the app sends one request to count the install. It carries no id, no machine name and no headers of its own. HiveHub counts the same plugin from one address once a day.
- A plugin installed from a folder is never counted.

## What leaves your computer

| What | Where to | Turn it off |
|---|---|---|
| The update check | GitHub, for new versions of the app | **Settings → About and updates → Check for new versions** |
| The plugin list | HiveHub, for new versions of the agents it adds for you | The same switch. You still reach HiveHub when you browse plugins yourself. |
| Traffic to your other devices and guests | Directly, or through the relay of your own [network server](/self-hosting) | Stay on *Local network* in **Settings → Servers** |

With the update check off, the app reaches nothing your network does not name, until you browse plugins yourself. Your agents' own programs talk to their providers as they always do.

## Before you let someone in

Both screens show three words. If they differ, someone sits between you: press **Deny**. An
invite link gives at most `terminals`. How: [Share a workspace](/multiplayer).
