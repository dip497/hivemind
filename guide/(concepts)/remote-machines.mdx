title: "Machines and hosts"
description: "How Hivemind runs terminals and agents on another computer: a machine over ssh, or a host over hive-net."
---

A frame can sit on another computer. Its terminals and agents then run there. There are two ways
to reach it.

<Diagram name="machines-paths" caption="Two ways to run on another machine" />

- **Your computer** → **machine**: ssh, with your own `~/.ssh/config` and keys. With **Install hive there** on, the app puts its own version of `hive` there and runs `hive daemon bridge`, which joins your own **background process** on that machine. Each person who logs in as the same user has their own.
- **Your computer** → **host**: hive-net, directly or through your relay. The host runs `hive host`, whose background process runs the workspace's terminals and agents.
- A machine without `hive`, which is how one starts and every Windows one stays, runs plain ssh terminals. They end when the connection drops: see [Keep terminals running](/machines#keep-terminals-running).

## Machine or host

| | Machine | Host |
|---|---|---|
| Holds the workspace | no: your computer does | yes, after **Move there** |
| Reached by guests and your phone | through your computer | directly, while your computer is off |
| Set it up | [Run agents on another computer](/machines) | [Keep agents running while your laptop sleeps](/host) |
